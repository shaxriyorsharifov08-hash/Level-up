📘 HUNTER'S MANUAL — HOW THE SYSTEM WORKS

This app turns your real life into an RPG. Real actions earn XP, XP raises your level, levels raise your rank — and the System watches everything: it rewards discipline and punishes absence.

⚡ XP, LEVELS & RANKS
  • Earning XP: complete quests. Harder tiers pay more — EASY 15 · MEDIUM 35 · EPIC 75 · MYTHIC 140 · LEGENDARY 250 XP. Clearing the whole Daily Quest package adds a +60 XP bonus.
  • Leveling: when the XP bar fills, you level up and receive +3 stat points plus whatever the Level Road grants at that level.
  • Ranks: E → D (Lv.5) → C (Lv.10) → B (Lv.15) → A (Lv.20) → S (Lv.30) → NATIONAL (Lv.50). Rank-ups bring titles, badges, a special Rank Trial quest, and your hero art evolves.
  • Stats: INT · STR · CON · END. Each quest trains the stat you assign it. Spend unallocated points on PROFILE. Every 5 levels the System checks your weakest stat and assigns a corrective quest.
📜 QUESTS & THE ROUND BUTTON
  • Types: DAILY (every day), WEEKLY (once per week), CUSTOM (chosen weekdays), ONE-TIME GOAL (finish it once), CHALLENGE (streak-based).
  • The round button: tap once = fully complete (filled ✓, full XP). Tap again = half complete (½, half the XP returned). Tap a third time = cleared, as if never done. Only today's completion can be adjusted.
  • Completion target: every quest has a target (e.g. 7). Each completion counts toward it; reaching it fully completes the quest and earns 1 roll. Recurring quests then start a new cycle.
  • Schedule (goals & challenges): optional start date (locked until then), deadline (countdown, then EXPIRED), and repeat (reopens N days after completion).
  • Focus timer: press ⏱ on a quest card before working, STOP when done. Time is logged per quest and per day, and appears in STATS.
  • ⏱ Timer-required quests: tick "Timer required" when creating a quest and it can only be completed after a tracked session that day — no timer, no clear.
  • 🚧 Priority lock (one-time goals): an EPIC lock seals easy+medium quests, MYTHIC seals every quest in the log, LEGENDARY seals everything including the Daily Quest — until the goal is done.
  • 🎒 Item boosts: press 🎒 on a quest card to arm a skill or equipment from your inventory; completing the quest consumes it and fires its bonus (2× XP, +1 random roll, or +1 stat point — you set each item's bonus yourself).
🔥 CHALLENGES

A challenge is a streak contract: complete it every day, and the unbroken streak IS the progress. Miss one day and progress resets to zero — you start again. Survive to the target and it is conquered forever: a roll, a badge, and a trophy in the Hall of Honor.

📦 DAILY QUEST (SYSTEM MANDATE)

The package on your home page resets every day at 01:00. Clear every item for +60 XP, a MEDIUM roll, and a mandate streak. Edit the items in ADMIN. Ignore it and the Penalty Zone opens…

🎁 REWARDS, ROLLS & CRATES
  • Crates (collections): you create them — name, tier, and 2–8 prizes you define. Link quests to a crate.
  • Rolls: earned by fully completing quests (linked quest → roll for its crate; unlinked → roll for any crate of that tier). Rolls wait in STORAGE until you press USE — then a random prize from the crate is revealed, gacha style.
  • Completing a crate (winning every prize) triggers a celebration and can grant a title.
🗺 STORY — LEVEL ROAD

The winding road on the STORY page shows every level ahead, passing landmark stops like the Pyramids and the Colosseum. Each node carries awards granted automatically on level-up: rolls, stat points, skills, equipment, food, titles, or a prize you pick from the list (or write yourself). Edit everything in ADMIN → STORY ROAD AWARDS. Story chapters unlock as your total completions grow.

🏆 HALL OF HONOR

Every achieved one-time goal, conquered challenge, and passed rank trial becomes a permanent trophy: ribbon, tier glow, campaign length, time invested, and the date. The finished quest leaves the quest log the next day — its place is in the hall.

⛓ PUNISHMENTS
  • Penalty Quest: miss the Daily Quest package and the Penalty Zone opens — all XP is frozen until you serve the penalty task and confirm it.
  • XP Debt: each fully empty day adds 60 XP of debt; half of every gain is seized until repaid.
  • Quest Lockdown: two consecutive empty days seal 2 random quests for 7 days.
☁ DATA & SYNC
  • Local: progress saves instantly in your browser, with an IndexedDB vault and 14 days of snapshots as backup.
  • Cloud: sign in with Google (ADMIN → CLOUD SYNC) to auto-sync across devices. Use UPLOAD/DOWNLOAD there for first-time setup direction.
  • Backup: ADMIN → EXPORT JSON saves everything to a file; IMPORT restores it anywhere.
📱 INSTALL & OFFLINE

Add the site to your home screen (browser menu → Add to Home Screen) and it installs like a real app with its own icon. The app works fully offline — everything except cloud sync and charts keeps running with no internet, and your progress syncs when you're back online.

📇 PROFILE EXTRAS

The dossier stores your height, weight, age, status, and real-life achievements (IELTS, CEFR, certificates). Skills, equipment and food earned from the Level Road collect in your 🎒 INVENTORY — tap any item to USE it (it clears one remaining Daily Quest task and is consumed) or remove it. Click your hunter name to rename yourself; upload per-rank portraits in ADMIN.
